# Mob animations v1

Built-in imagegen generated 32 transparent source sheets: `{cultist,skeleton,acolyte,hound}-motion-{direction}-v1.png` in this folder. Eight independently authored views each contain eight walking poses (0–7), a planted standing pose (8), and seven attack poses (9–15). Original designs remain in mob-reference/. Source PNGs are never modified by fitting/compilation.

Runtime: distance-driven strides (cultist/acolyte 64, skeleton 60, hound 76 world pixels), grounded boot/paw anchors, stable 45-degree facing selection, retained gait phase on stopping, and simulation-owned 0.45-second one-shot attack playback. Attack direction is captured toward the hero at the original damage event; attacks take visual priority over walking. Damage still happens at its original instant, with no added windup delay. The archer retains the existing contact-attack behavior. AI, stats, waves, collision, rewards, inheritance, campsite companions and rival competition remain unchanged.

`compile-mob-atlases.cjs` serializes the shared 2× body-resolution canvas cache to mob-runtime/ for smaller offline/mobile embedding. Original full-resolution artwork is retained for review. Only a mob design encountered in rendering is decoded; Image sources are released after caching. Humanoid body height is 54 world pixels; hounds 38 including crystals. Eight attack directions are authored without runtime equipment mirroring.

Rebuild: `python prepare-mob-animation.py`, `node compile-mob-atlases.cjs`, `node integrate.cjs`, `node make-mob-preview.cjs`. Review `mob-animation-review.html`; contact review `node review-mob-contact.cjs`. Tests: `node test-mob-animation.cjs` and full package suite. Desktop/mobile Chromium emulation is tested; physical iPhone Safari remains unverified.
